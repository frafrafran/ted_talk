Inside Harry's mind

The Mirror of Erised

We don't just watch Harry. We are Harry.

On screen

An actor's face and a piece of music.

On the page

Pages inside his head: his longing, his confusion, his fear of losing it.

(0:50–2:00) In the book, we don't just watch Harry. We are Harry. We feel his loneliness in the cupboard under the stairs. We live inside his thoughts when he first sees the Mirror of Erised and finds his parents smiling back at him. On screen, that moment depends on an actor's face and a piece of music. It's beautiful. But in the book, you spend pages inside his head, feeling his longing, his confusion, his fear of losing it. [Next slide: window and door.]
