Peeves, the troublemaking poltergeist, was cut completely. [The "Cut from film" stamp slams onto his card.] And when a story gets shortened, characters become shortcuts: Hermione is "the smart one," Neville is "the clumsy one." In the book, they have room to be human. That isn't the filmmakers' fault. It's the rule of the medium. But something is always lost.
