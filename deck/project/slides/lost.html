Point II · Lost in adaptation

When a story shrinks, characters become shortcuts

Not in the film

Peeves

The poltergeist, cut completely.

Hermione

On screen: “the smart one.”

Neville

On screen: “the clumsy one.”

In the book, they have room to be human.

Peeves, the troublemaking poltergeist, was cut completely. And when a story gets shortened, characters become shortcuts: Hermione is "the smart one," Neville is "the clumsy one." In the book, they have room to be human. That isn't the filmmakers' fault. It's the rule of the medium. But something is always lost.
