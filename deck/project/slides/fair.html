(3:40–4:15) To be fair, film does things books can't. John Williams' music gives you chills before anyone says a word. The casting was so perfect that Hagrid will always be Robbie Coltrane. And Quidditch in motion is thrilling. But notice: the film adds to the story. It doesn't replace it. The story was born in the book.
